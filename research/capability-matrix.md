## 1. The product in one paragraph (true today)

OpenFrame Studio is a local-first desktop app for writers, screenwriters and production teams
(`W:\apps\desktop\src-tauri\tauri.conf.json:39`). One project folder holds the Idea Vault, the Story (a Story page and a
Beat Board), one or more screenplays, the breakdown and catalog, locations, cast and crew, moodboards, storyboards, shot
lists, a shooting schedule with Day Out of Days, Daily View, sides, reports, call sheets and a light budget. It runs on
Windows 11 and on Apple silicon Macs (macOS 14 or later). There is no account, no cloud service and no telemetry. An
optional offline AI assistant runs on the computer after a one-time model download of about 1.2 GB. Today it exists only
as unsigned internal builds behind a team password; there is no public download, no price and no trial running.
