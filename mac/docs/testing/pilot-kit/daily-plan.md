# Daily plan: 3 days

Each day: 30 to 60 minutes of real work in the app, then the short checklist. Use a copy of a script, never your only copy.
Write a bug report for anything wrong ([template](../bug-report-template.md)). One problem per report. Do not send your
real script.

## First install on your laptop (clean-machine checks)

Your laptop is a clean machine, so these checks are part of the proof. Do them once, on day 1, and write the result next to
each line. Also write down your laptop brand and model name (read it from the sticker or the box), whether the AI
download finished, and roughly how long it took.

- [ ] The installer ran with no administrator prompt.
- [ ] I got past the "Windows protected your PC" window with the steps in the [install note](install-note.md).
- [ ] The app opened straight to Home, with no password.
- [ ] The AI download finished.
- [ ] With Wi-Fi off, the assistant still answered.
- [ ] I exported a PDF.
- [ ] I made a backup and restored it.

On day 3, also do this:
- [ ] I uninstalled the app. The message said my projects are kept.
- [ ] The Documents\OpenFrame folder was still there.
- [ ] I reinstalled the app.
- [ ] With Wi-Fi off, I started it and my project opened.

## Day 1: writing

Work (30 to 60 minutes):
- Create a project. Close it. Reopen it from Home.
- Idea Vault: add a note, an image, a link. Send one item to the Beat Board.
- Beat Board: make acts and scene cards. Reorder them.
- Screenplay: type scenes with headings, action, dialogue, parentheticals and transitions. Use Undo and Redo.
- Close the window right after typing. Reopen. Check every word is there.
- Import a Final Draft (FDX) or Fountain file you already have, as a copy.
- Export to PDF. Open the PDF.

Checklist:
- [ ] Nothing I typed was lost.
- [ ] The import kept scenes, character names, dialogue and title page.
- [ ] The PDF looks right.
- [ ] I wrote a report for every problem.

## Day 2: production

Work (30 to 60 minutes):
- Breakdown: tag props, wardrobe and locations in a few scenes. Accept and reject suggestions.
- Schedule: make two shooting days. Move scenes between days. Add a meal break.
- Call sheets: make one from a day. Change the schedule and check the call sheet asks for a refresh.
- Exports: try each export you find (schedule, call sheet, breakdown, script).
- Backup and restore: Create Backup, then restore it as a copy. Check the copy is complete.

Checklist:
- [ ] Moving a scene updated the schedule and the call sheet warning.
- [ ] Every export opened in its normal program.
- [ ] The restored copy had all my work.
- [ ] I wrote a report for every problem.

## Day 3: AI assistant and stress

Work (30 to 60 minutes):
- Ask the assistant in English: list the scenes in one location, rename a character, make a task.
- Ask again in your own language: Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese or
  Korean. Note when the answer comes in the wrong language or reads badly.
- Read the Proposed Changes card. Apply one change, undo it, reject another. The AI must never change your project
  before you apply.
- Switch off the network for ten minutes and use the app, including the AI.
- Close the app and reopen it. Put the computer to sleep and wake it.
- Optional, skip it if you are not sure how: while typing, end the app from Task Manager (End task). Reopen. Choose the option that keeps your
  latest work. Check what you typed.
- Export Diagnostics and Export AI report ([how to send logs](how-to-send-logs.md)).

Checklist:
- [ ] The AI changed nothing until I applied a proposal.
- [ ] Answers in my language were right, or I reported them.
- [ ] Offline use worked.
- [ ] After a forced close, my latest text came back.
- [ ] I filled in the end-of-pilot questions in the [test plan](../internal-test-plan.md) section 9.
