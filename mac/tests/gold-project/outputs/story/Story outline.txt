    Scene Card 3 · INT. SOUNDWAVE STUDIO - BENGALURU - DAY

    Scene Card 11 · INT. COAST RADIO STATION - OPERATIONS ROOM - NIGHT

    Scene Card 12 · EXT. COAST RADIO STATION - NIGHT
