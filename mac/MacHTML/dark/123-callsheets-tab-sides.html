NIGHT BUS · SIDES · SHOOT DAY 1 · Example-Short (imported)
1. INT. KESARGANJ JUNCTION - LOST PROPERTY OFFICE - NIGHT FADE IN: A small office behind a barred window. Shelves of forgotten things: umbrellas, a single shoe, a birdcage with no bird, a box of keys labelled by year. DEVI (60s, cardigan over a railway uniform, reading glasses) eats peanuts from a paper cone and fills in a REGISTER in slow, neat capitals. A knock on the window bars. She looks up. KIRAN (12, school bag, hair wet from the washroom tap) stands on tiptoe to see over the counter. KIRAN Did anyone hand in a kite? DEVI What kind of kite? KIRAN Red. A long tail made of newspaper. It says KIRAN on the back in marker. Devi runs a finger down the register. DEVI No kites. One goat, last Tuesday. He was collected.
